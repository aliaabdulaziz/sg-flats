# Data attribution and third-party notices

New school/food/grocery/park sources remain research candidates under the [amenity audit](amenity-audit.md). Their full-file reuse and redistribution terms must be recorded before adoption; no newly downloaded amenity data is claimed here.

The coordinate research uses HDB Existing Building and resale/property information from HDB via data.gov.sg, under the [Singapore Open Data Licence](https://data.gov.sg/open-data-licence), accessed 5 November 2026. Transport geometry and station codes are from LTA DataMall, accessed 5 November 2026. These agencies do not endorse SG Flats.

The additional NUS comparison is from [NUS Urban Analytics Lab's hdb3d-data](https://github.com/ualsg/hdb3d-data), dataset version 2019-08-25, repository commit `4a87d9c2811ea43969d9788a5cb4c9a55a977ad9`. Its authors request attribution to **NUS Urban Analytics Lab, HDB Singapore, OpenStreetMap contributors, and OneMap**. Their work documents known footprint-association errors; SG Flats uses geometric corroboration and excludes conflicts. The repository reports MIT licensing, reproduced below.
